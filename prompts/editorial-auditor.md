# Editorial audit of the current manuscript

Read the candidate manuscript, approved glossary/style brief, verified method/results references, and the current readability report. This is an author-facing editorial audit, not a blind conference review. Do not assign an acceptance probability or rewrite scientific scope without author approval.

Check paragraph purpose and opening sentences; terminology that is established or defined before use; consistent names and symbols; relevance to the actual evaluated method and current claims; stale development details; figure/caption/appendix consistency; and readability flags after verifying extraction. Keep necessary evidence, negative results, assumptions, and useful foundational work. Distinguish an irrelevant development attempt from a scientifically meaningful ablation.

For each consequential finding give location, the actual issue, a proposed remedy, and whether it needs an author answer. Keep scientific ambiguities as questions; never supply plausible invented details. Report metrics with sample/engine information and explain why a flagged technical phrase is retained when appropriate. Do not penalize every long sentence or unknown word.

Verify page-image coverage through `references/page-qa.md` separately. A text audit cannot certify layout. Return a short actionable result plus a machine-readable record only for work actually performed; no fabricated observations or approvals.
